| **Approach** | **Key assumptions** | **Target causal quantity** | **Typical software** | **What to report** | **Sensitivity tool** |
|---------------|---------------------|-----------------------------|----------------------|--------------------|----------------------|
| Counterfactual mediation (Valeri & VanderWeele, 2013; VanderWeele, 2016) | Sequential ignorability (for exposure→mediator and mediator→outcome); consistency; positivity; correct link/functional form | NDE, NIE/ACME (ADE) on risk-difference (RD) or risk-ratio (RR) scales | R: `mediation`, Stata: `paramed` / `g-formula` | DAG, covariate set, model family/link; ACME/ADE with CIs; emphasize RD, RR as complement | R: `medsens()`, E-values (VanderWeele & Ding, 2017) |
| Four-way decomposition (VanderWeele, 2014) | As above + explicit X×M interaction; interpret on additive (RD) scale | TE = CDE + INT_ref + INT_med + PIE (on RD scale) | Stata: `med4way`, R: `CMAverse::cmest()` | Report CDE, INT_ref, INT_med, PIE with CIs; specify reference levels | Bootstrap/delta CIs; E-values |
| Interventional (in)direct effects (Tchetgen Tchetgen & VanderWeele, 2014) | Allows mediator–outcome confounders affected by exposure; correct models for interventional distributions | Interventional direct and indirect effects (policy-amenable contrasts) | R: `CMAverse`, `medflex`; Stata: `g-computation` | Define interventions; align with policy lever; RD/RR summaries | Simulation-based checks; E-values |
| SEM / (G)SEM (Rosseel, 2012; De Stavola et al., 2015) | Parametric structure; identification via exclusions/anchors; valid latent measurement model | Path coefficients; implied indirect effects; latent-variable scores | R: `lavaan`; Stata: `sem` / `gsem` | Measurement model (loadings, fit indices); cross-validated fit | Model robustness; E-values |
